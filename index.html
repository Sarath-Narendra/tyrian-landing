Capabilities

06 // SYSTEMS ONLINE
01

Voice Controlled

Natural-language voice control. Talk to your tools the way you'd talk to a partner — no wake words, no scripts.

02

Part Handling

Servo-driven gripper. Handles fasteners, PCBs, and odd-shaped fabricated parts without the spreadsheet of grippers.

03

Visual Recognition

Stereo depth + transformer vision. Identifies tools, anomalies, and finishes — and remembers what you keep on your bench.

04

Cloud Sync

Project state, build logs, and learned skills sync across your fleet. Walk to another bench, pick up where you stopped.

05

OTA Updates

Your robot gets sharper monthly. Signed firmware, instant rollbacks, and a public changelog you can actually read.

06

Tool Holding

Swap drivers, calipers, irons, even a coffee. Quick-release coupler keyed for fast, repeatable tool changes.
